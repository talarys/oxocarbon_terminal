OXOCARBON — GENERIC PALETTE REFERENCE
====================================

Source: https://github.com/nyoom-engineering/oxocarbon.nvim
Reference implementation: oxocarbon.nvim (dark + light).
The gray ramp is generated in HSLuv between base00 and base06; the values
below are the resolved output, not approximations.

IMPORTANT: Oxocarbon uses Base16 *slots* but not Base16 *semantics*.
Unlike a typical Base16 theme, base08 is not red and base0B is not green.
Always use the explicit role names / ANSI mapping below when writing a port.

NOTES FOR PORT AUTHORS
----------------------
- The ANSI mapping above follows the official Nyoom terminal ports
  (oxocarbon-alacritty / oxocarbon-wezterm), not nvim's terminal_color_*
  values. The nvim mapping reuses blue/purple/green for red/yellow, which is
  faithful to the scheme but confusing in a shell.
- Yellow #ffe97b exists only in the terminal ports; it is not in the Neovim
  palette. Add it as an extension rather than forcing it into a Base16 slot.
- Prefer foreground #d0d0d0 (dark) / #37474f (light) over pure white/black;
  those are the exact Normal foregrounds.
- The light palette's pastel ANSI accents are low-contrast on #ffffff
  (roughly 2.3-3.3:1 for green, cyan, yellow, red). That is faithful to the
  upstream light palette. Dark is the high-contrast option.
